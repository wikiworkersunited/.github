Wiki Workers United
===================
<img src="img/logo.png" width="100" align="left" alt="Wiki Workers United logo, a rased fist on a field of green surrounded by the red and blue arc of the Wikimedia community logo">

We are Wiki Workers United, a global union for the staff of the Wikimedia
Foundation. We are the people who do the work to provide a platform, services,
and funds to support the Wikimedia movement.
